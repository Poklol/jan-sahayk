# Andaman and Nicobar Islands Official Welfare Schemes

This document contains official welfare schemes mapped to Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

## Andaman and Nicobar Islands State Housing Program (Awas Yojana)
Scheme Type: housing_support
Good Match Signals: low income, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, rural, urban poor
Summary: Providing financial assistance for constructing permanent pucca houses for eligible residents in Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
Eligibility: Must be a resident of Andaman and Nicobar Islands. Family income must be below 2 lakh per annum. Priority given to SC, ST, and BPL categories. Applicant should not own a permanent house.
Benefits: Financial assistance up to ₹1.2 Lakh for rural and up to ₹2.5 Lakh for urban households, disbursed in three installments directly to the bank account.
Application Steps: Visit the local Panchayat or Municipal office, verify BPL status, and submit land ownership documents. 
Official Link: https://andaman_and_nicobar_islands.gov.in/housing

## Andaman and Nicobar Islands Farmer Support Scheme (Krishi Sahayata)
Scheme Type: farmer_support, agriculture, loan_support
Good Match Signals: farmer, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, agricultural loan, crop
Summary: Comprehensive financial and input subsidy support for farmers registered in Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
Eligibility: Marginal or small farmers residing in Andaman and Nicobar Islands owning culturable land up to 2 hectares. 
Benefits: Direct benefit transfer of ₹6000 per year per family. Subsidized crop loans at 0% interest if repaid on time. Input subsidies for seeds and fertilizers.
Application Steps: Register on the Andaman and Nicobar Islands Agriculture Portal with Aadhar and land records (Khata/Khasra).
Official Link: https://agri.andaman_and_nicobar_islands.gov.in

## Andaman and Nicobar Islands Student Scholarship and Education Aid
Scheme Type: scholarship_support
Good Match Signals: student, education, college, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, sc, st, obc
Summary: Financial assistance for higher education for underprivileged students.
Eligibility: Resident of Andaman and Nicobar Islands pursuing pre-matric or post-matric education. Annual family income strictly below 2.5 lakh. SC, ST, or OBC students are highly prioritized.
Benefits: Full tuition fee waiver and an annual stipend of ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 depending on the course of study.
Application Steps: Apply via the Andaman and Nicobar Islands State Scholarship Portal with income and caste certificates every academic year.
Official Link: https://scholarships.andaman_and_nicobar_islands.gov.in

## Andaman and Nicobar Islands Old Age Pension Scheme
Scheme Type: pension_support
Good Match Signals: elderly, pension, senior citizen, Andaman and Nicobar Islands
Summary: Monthly pension assistance for destitute senior citizens.
Eligibility: Must be over 60 years of age (55 for females in certain specific backward districts). Must be below the poverty line (BPL).
Benefits: A monthly pension of ₹1000 to ₹2500 credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account.
Application Steps: Apply through the Social Welfare Department in Andaman and Nicobar Islands using the online service portal or at the district collectorate.
Official Link: https://socialwelfare.andaman_and_nicobar_islands.gov.in
