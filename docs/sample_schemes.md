## PM-KISAN
Scheme Type: farmer_support, income_support
Summary: PM-KISAN is a central income-support scheme for eligible landholding farmer families.
Target Group: Landholding farmer families, subject to scheme exclusions and state record verification.
Eligibility: Applicant should be part of a landholding farmer family recorded in state or UT land records. Institutional landholders and several excluded categories such as certain income-tax payers or constitutional post holders are not eligible.
Benefits: Financial assistance of Rs 6,000 per year is released in three equal installments, directly to eligible beneficiaries.
Application Steps: Use the PM-KISAN portal, Common Service Centre, or state agriculture machinery for registration and status updates. Aadhaar and land-record validation are important checkpoints.
Documents Often Needed: Aadhaar, bank account details, land record details, mobile number.
Good Match Signals: farmer, landholding, installment support, direct benefit transfer, agriculture household.
Important Notes: Final approval depends on state-verified beneficiary data and exclusion checks.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
Scheme Type: farmer_support, loan_support, credit_support
Summary: Kisan Credit Card gives farmers a formal credit line for crop production and allied activities.
Target Group: Farmers, including cultivators and eligible applicants involved in agriculture and allied activities.
Eligibility: Applicants generally need farmer identification and bank-level assessment. Coverage usually extends to crop cultivation and may include allied activities depending on banking norms and scheme rules.
Benefits: Provides revolving credit support for crop and related farm needs through the KCC framework, helping farmers access institutional credit instead of informal borrowing.
Application Steps: Apply through participating banks with farmer identity details, land or cultivation details, and bank-required forms. Bank verification determines sanction.
Documents Often Needed: Identity proof, address proof, land or cultivation details, photographs, bank forms.
Good Match Signals: crop loan, credit for farmers, seasonal agriculture expenses, working capital for cultivation.
Important Notes: Final credit limit, margin, and bank-specific documentation are determined by the lending institution.
Official Source: https://www.nabard.org/content1.aspx?id=602&catid=23&mid=530
Official Source 2: https://pmkisan.gov.in/
Verified On: 2026-04-09

## Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)
Scheme Type: farmer_support, pension_support
Summary: PM-KMY is a voluntary pension scheme for eligible small and marginal farmers.
Target Group: Small and marginal farmers in the eligible entry-age bracket.
Eligibility: Entry age is typically 18 to 40 years. The applicant should qualify under the scheme conditions for small and marginal farmers and should not fall under the listed exclusion categories.
Benefits: The scheme is designed to provide a minimum assured monthly pension of Rs 3,000 after the beneficiary reaches 60 years of age, subject to contribution rules.
Application Steps: Enroll through Common Service Centres or approved channels linked with the official scheme portal. Aadhaar, bank details, and landholding details are typically used in onboarding.
Documents Often Needed: Aadhaar, bank details, landholding information, mobile number.
Good Match Signals: farmer pension, old-age support for farmers, contribution-based pension, age 18 to 40.
Important Notes: This is a contribution-based pension program; eligibility and continuation depend on scheme conditions and contribution compliance.
Official Source: https://maandhan.in/
Official Source 2: https://pmkisan.gov.in/
Verified On: 2026-04-09

## Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)
Scheme Type: housing_support, rural_support
Summary: PMAY-G supports eligible rural households that lack adequate pucca housing.
Target Group: Rural households identified through approved deprivation or survey-based beneficiary processes.
Eligibility: Final eligibility is linked to rural housing deprivation data, Gram Sabha validation, and PMAY-G workflow rules. The scheme is not meant for applicants who already have adequate pucca housing under the applicable criteria.
Benefits: Provides rural housing assistance under the PMAY-G framework, along with sanctioned workflow support through the official rural housing system.
Application Steps: Check eligibility and beneficiary status through the PMAY-G ecosystem or local rural development authorities. Beneficiary selection is not an open self-declared approval system; it depends on verified rural lists and official processes.
Documents Often Needed: Aadhaar, bank details, rural household identification details, local verification records.
Good Match Signals: rural house, pucca house, kutcha housing, housing support in village areas.
Important Notes: Final selection depends on official beneficiary lists, survey data, and local validation.
Official Source: https://pmayg.nic.in/
Official Source 2: https://rhreporting.nic.in/
Verified On: 2026-04-09

## Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)
Scheme Type: health_support, insurance_support
Summary: AB PM-JAY is a government-funded health assurance scheme for eligible families.
Target Group: Eligible families identified under the PM-JAY criteria and approved beneficiary databases.
Eligibility: Eligibility is based on the PM-JAY beneficiary database rather than open income self-declaration alone. Users should verify family eligibility using the official beneficiary-check channels.
Benefits: Provides health cover for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization through an empanelled hospital network under the official PM-JAY framework.
Application Steps: Verify eligibility using the official beneficiary-check process, then access empanelled hospitals with valid identity and scheme-linked records.
Documents Often Needed: Aadhaar or other accepted ID, family verification details, mobile number.
Good Match Signals: hospital cashless support, government health cover, major hospitalization expenses, low-income family health treatment.
Important Notes: Coverage and package use depend on eligibility verification and empanelled-network rules.
Official Source: https://nha.gov.in/PM-JAY.php
Official Source 2: https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/
Verified On: 2026-04-09

## National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
Scheme Type: scholarship_support, education_support
Summary: NSP is the central portal used for many central and state scholarship workflows.
Target Group: Students applying under participating scholarship schemes listed on the portal.
Eligibility: Eligibility depends on the specific scholarship selected. Common filters include course level, category, disability status, family income ceiling, academic performance, domicile, and institution requirements.
Benefits: The portal enables application, document submission, verification, and tracking for multiple scholarship schemes rather than one single universal benefit.
Application Steps: Register on the National Scholarship Portal, choose the relevant scheme, complete profile and academic details, upload required documents, and track verification and sanction status.
Documents Often Needed: Aadhaar or alternative ID as allowed, income certificate, caste certificate when applicable, mark sheets, bank details, institution verification details.
Good Match Signals: scholarship, student support, tuition support, hosteller scholarship, minority scholarship, pre-matric, post-matric.
Important Notes: Final benefit amount and conditions vary by scholarship. Users must choose the exact scheme that fits their category and course.
Official Source: https://scholarships.gov.in/
Verified On: 2026-04-09

## Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
Scheme Type: insurance_support, life_cover
Summary: PMJJBY is a low-cost life insurance scheme linked to participating bank accounts.
Target Group: Eligible bank-account holders in the applicable age range.
Eligibility: Entry age is generally 18 to 50 years through a participating bank or post office, with continuation rules under the scheme terms.
Benefits: Provides life insurance cover of Rs 2 lakh on death, subject to scheme terms and successful enrollment.
Application Steps: Enroll through a participating bank or post office, usually via consent-based auto-debit and scheme form submission.
Documents Often Needed: Savings account details, Aadhaar or KYC-linked bank records, consent form.
Good Match Signals: low-cost life insurance, family financial protection, accidental-free natural death coverage not tied to hospitalization.
Important Notes: Premium, renewal cycle, and account status rules matter. Users should maintain an active linked bank account.
Official Source: https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in/
Official Source 2: https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in/Files/PMJJBY/English/Rules.pdf

## Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
Scheme Type: pension_support, unorganised_worker_support
Summary: APY is a contributory pension scheme focused on long-term income security after age 60.
Target Group: Eligible subscribers, especially workers seeking a small-ticket long-term pension arrangement.
Eligibility: Entry age is generally 18 to 40 years. The subscriber contributes periodically until age 60, subject to scheme rules.
Benefits: Offers a choice of guaranteed minimum monthly pension slabs after age 60, subject to the selected contribution tier and scheme conditions.
Application Steps: Enroll through a participating bank or post office with an eligible savings account and complete the APY registration workflow.
Documents Often Needed: Savings account details, Aadhaar when linked or required, mobile number, consent for auto-debit.
Good Match Signals: old-age pension planning, unorganised worker pension, long-term small contribution pension.
Important Notes: APY is contribution-based. Benefit level depends on the selected pension slab and the continuity of contributions.
Official Source: https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in/
Official Source 2: https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in/scheme-details.php
Verified On: 2026-04-09

## Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM)
Scheme Type: pension_support, unorganised_worker_support
Summary: PM-SYM is a voluntary contributory pension scheme for unorganised workers.
Target Group: Unorganised workers in the eligible age and income bracket.
Eligibility: Entry age is generally 18 to 40 years, and the worker should satisfy the income and unorganised-sector criteria defined by the scheme.
Benefits: The scheme is designed to provide a minimum assured monthly pension of Rs 3,000 after age 60, subject to contribution compliance.
Application Steps: Enroll through Common Service Centres or approved scheme channels using Aadhaar, bank details, and mobile-linked verification.
Documents Often Needed: Aadhaar, savings bank account or Jan Dhan account, mobile number.
Good Match Signals: labourer pension, street worker pension, unorganised sector retirement support, age 18 to 40.
Important Notes: This is a contribution-based scheme. Enrollment rules and continuity matter.
Official Source: https://maandhan.in/
Official Source 2: https://www.eshram.gov.in/social-security-welfare-schemes

## eShram Registration
Scheme Type: unorganised_worker_support, labour_support
Summary: eShram is a national database and identity-linked registration platform for unorganised workers.
Target Group: Unorganised workers across sectors such as construction, domestic work, agriculture labour, gig work, and other informal occupations.
Eligibility: The worker should fall within the unorganised sector definition used by the portal and complete registration with the required identity details.
Benefits: eShram registration itself is not a single cash scheme, but it helps create an authenticated worker record and can support access, discovery, and policy linkage for welfare measures.
Application Steps: Register on the eShram portal or through assisted channels using Aadhaar-linked details and occupational information.
Documents Often Needed: Aadhaar, mobile number, bank account details, occupation details.
Good Match Signals: unorganised worker ID, informal labour registration, gig worker registration, construction worker registration.
Important Notes: eShram works best as a foundational worker record and not as a standalone pension or insurance substitute.
Official Source: https://eshram.gov.in/
Official Source 2: https://www.eshram.gov.in/social-security-welfare-schemes
Verified On: 2026-04-09

## Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)
Scheme Type: pension_support, elderly_support
Summary: IGNOAPS is part of the National Social Assistance Programme for eligible elderly persons from poor households.
Target Group: Elderly persons from eligible low-income households under NSAP rules.
Eligibility: Eligibility is linked to age thresholds and the NSAP household-poverty criteria defined by the program and implemented through states and UTs.
Benefits: Provides old-age pension support under NSAP, with central assistance and possible state top-ups depending on the jurisdiction.
Application Steps: Apply through the local social welfare, panchayat, or district-level implementation channel used by the state or UT.
Documents Often Needed: Age proof, identity proof, address proof, household or poverty-status proof as required locally, bank details.
Good Match Signals: old age pension, elderly support, pension for poor senior citizens, social assistance pension.
Important Notes: Final amount may vary because states often add their own contribution above the central assistance component.
Official Source: https://nsap.nic.in/
Official Source 2: https://www.eshram.gov.in/social-security-welfare-schemes
